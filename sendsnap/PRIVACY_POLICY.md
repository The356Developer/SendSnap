# Privacy Policy for SendSnap

**Last Updated: November 21, 2026**

Welcome to **SendSnap - Fast WiFi File Transfer**. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application (the "Service").

---

## 1. Data Collection and Usage

### Personal Information
SendSnap does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal information (such as your name, email address, or phone number) to our servers.

### File Transfers
All file transfers performed through SendSnap occur directly between devices over your local WiFi network or Wi-Fi Direct peer-to-peer connection. We do **not** upload your files to any cloud server or third-party service. Your data stays entirely within your local environment.

### Local Discovery
The app uses local network discovery (UDP/mDNS) and Wi-Fi Direct service discovery to detect nearby devices. This discovery process is strictly local and does not involve any external tracking.

### Advertising & Analytics
SendSnap contains **no advertisements** and does **not** integrate any third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs (and does not access your device Advertising ID).

---

## 2. Permissions Required

To provide our service, SendSnap requires the following permissions on your device:

*   **Storage / Photos & Videos**: Necessary to select files to send and to save received files to your device.
*   **WiFi / Network State**: Required to establish a connection between devices for file transfer.
*   **Nearby Devices / Location**: (Android / iOS) Required by the operating system to scan for and connect to nearby devices over local Wi-Fi or Wi-Fi Direct. We do **not** track your physical location.

---

## 3. Children’s Privacy
Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

---

## 4. Security
We value your trust in using our Peer-to-Peer transfer service. Since transfers are local between your devices, they stay off the public internet. We recommend transferring files only with trusted devices on private networks.

---

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
